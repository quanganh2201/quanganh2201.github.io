Humanoids '26
Ukemi-SafeFall: Humanoid Safe Falling & Recovery
Quang Anh Vu et al.
IEEE-RAS International Conference on Humanoid Robots, 2026 under review
Website Video Sim clip
A PPO whole-body fall-and-recovery policy for a 23-DoF, 32 kg humanoid, trained in mjlab / MuJoCo with condition-gated impact-mitigation rewards and a Score-Matching Motion Prior, across 4,096 parallel environments with Generative State Initialization and domain randomization. In simulation it reaches 98% recovery success with a 1.04 s mean time-to-stand, and cuts normalized peak contact force by 32.1% and cumulative peak contact loading by 78.4% versus passive falling. Deployed on physical hardware with a 50 Hz C++ / ONNX Runtime control stack, recovering from kicks, obstacle traversal and elevated-platform falls.
ICRA '27
AR-FlowZ: Autoregressive Riemannian Latent Flows for Latent Skill Sequencing in Behavioral Foundation Models
Quang Anh Vu et al.
IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA), 2027 under review
Website Suit demo Camera demo
An autoregressive Riemannian latent-flow sequencer on top of a frozen FB-CPR behavioral foundation model. It generates smooth transitions between latent skills conditioned on state, previous latent, goal and recurrent history, with a SLERP-centered geometric prior on the normalized latent sphere. Distilling the multi-step flow into a one-step endpoint predictor cuts inference from about 100 ms to 3.4 ms (~29× faster), fitting the 20 ms budget of a 50 Hz loop. On a 46 s behavior-switching benchmark it improves transition success to 78.8% (vs. 75.9% direct readout) and lowers peak and p99 action rates by 18.8% and 15.8%. Deployed on a Digit humanoid through a ten-link Cartesian interface driven by a mocap suit or a monocular camera, with no per-frame inverse kinematics (mean tracking error 3.4° arms, 11.9° legs).
